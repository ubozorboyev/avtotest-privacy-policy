Last updated: 27.11.2025

AvtoTest does not collect, store, or process any personal information. This includes, but is not limited to:

Since AvtoTest does not collect any personal data:

AvtoTest may collect basic, non-personal technical information automatically for functionality and stability, such as:

This information cannot be used to identify you personally and is used only to improve app performance.

4. Children’s Privacy

AvtoTest does not collect any personal data from children or adults. It is suitable for users of all ages.
